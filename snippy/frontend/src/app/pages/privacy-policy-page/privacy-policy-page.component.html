Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what Snippy needs to work: your sign-in details, the snippets and files you create or upload, and basic server logs.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no third-party trackers. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • Uploaded files are public to anyone with the link, even if the snippet they belong to is private.
  • Snippy is not for children under 13. Teens 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian’s permission.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of this Snippy instance (“Snippy,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Snippy, an open-source tool for creating, previewing, and sharing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript snippets. It is part of our Terms and Conditions.

2. Information we collect

Snippy is built to collect as little as possible. This is the complete list.

  • Snippets and content you create. Your code, snippet titles, descriptions, tags, external library URLs, collections, comments, favorites, follows, forks, and editor preferences.
  • Files you upload. Assets (such as images, fonts, audio, and video), your avatar, and the preview snapshot images Snippy creates for your snippets, along with each file’s name, type, and size.
  • Account details needed to sign in. Your email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text), and the username Snippy creates for you. Display name and bio are optional. If you sign in with GitHub or Google, we receive an account identifier and your email address from that provider. We do not receive your password for that provider.
  • Basic activity counts. View, fork, favorite, comment, and embed counts on snippets, and a record of when you last viewed a snippet so repeat views are not double-counted.
  • Server logs. Like most websites, our server automatically records basic request information (such as IP address, browser type, page requested, and time) to keep the Service secure and working. These logs are kept for a short period, normally a few days, and then deleted automatically.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your real name, date of birth, phone number, or address.
  • We do not collect payment information.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or third-party tracking cookies.
  • We do not use your information to build advertising profiles.

4. Cookies and browser storage

Snippy does not use tracking cookies. It stores a few items in your browser’s local storage so the app works: your sign-in session, theme, editor layout, and unsaved drafts. This data stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time by signing out or clearing your browser’s site data.

5. How we use information

  • To provide Snippy: saving, previewing, sharing, forking, and organizing snippets.
  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as password resets and email verification. We do not send marketing email.
  • To show public content to others according to your settings.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce our Terms, fix problems, and keep the Service secure.
  • To comply with the law.

6. What other people can see

  • Uploaded files are public. Every uploaded file, including images, photos, avatars, assets, and preview snapshots of public snippets, can be opened by anyone who has its link, whether or not they are signed in. Using a file in a private snippet, collection, or profile does not make it private. Do not upload anything you would not want to be public. Preview snapshots of private snippets are the one exception: only you can open them while signed in.
  • Public content. Public snippets, collections, and comments, and public profiles (username, display name, bio, avatar, and follower counts) can be seen by anyone and may be indexed by search engines.
  • Private content. Private snippet code and private collections are shown only to you and to anyone you give a share link to.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

7. When information is shared

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers we use to run Snippy, such as our hosting provider and email delivery provider, only as needed to provide the Service.
  • With GitHub or Google, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • When we believe in good faith that the law requires it, or that it is needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of Snippy, our users, or the public, including reporting illegal content involving minors to the authorities.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of the Service, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

8. Third-party code in snippets

Snippets can load libraries, fonts, images, or other files from outside servers, such as public CDNs, chosen by the snippet’s author. When you open or preview such a snippet, your browser connects to those servers directly, and they may see your IP address and browser details. Their own privacy policies apply. We do not control them.

9. Children’s privacy (COPPA)

  • Snippy is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 may not create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information and the account as quickly as possible.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
  • Users aged 13 to 17 may use Snippy only with a parent or guardian’s permission. A parent or guardian may contact us to review, change, or delete their teen’s information, or to close the account.

10. Keeping and deleting your information

  • We keep your account and content while your account is active.
  • You can edit your profile, and delete individual snippets, collections, comments, and assets, at any time.
  • Deleting your account from Settings permanently deletes your account, snippets, collections, assets, and related data. Copies may remain in backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced.
  • Forks that other users made of your public snippets are their content and may remain, along with the original snippet name and author username shown on the fork.
  • We may keep limited information longer if the law requires it or to resolve disputes and enforce our Terms.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR and U.S. state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain uses. You can do most of this yourself in Settings, or contact us. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. Where GDPR applies, we process your information to provide the Service you asked for (performance of a contract), for our legitimate interests in keeping the Service secure, and to meet legal obligations.

12. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including hashed passwords, access rules on private content, and encrypted connections where the instance is served over HTTPS. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information. Never put passwords, API keys, or other secrets in a snippet.

13. Where information is stored

Your information is stored on the servers where this Snippy instance is hosted, which may be in a different country from yours, including the United States. By using Snippy, you understand that your information will be stored and processed there.

14. Self-hosted deployments

Snippy is open source, and anyone can host their own copy. If you are using an instance run by someone else, that operator controls your information and is responsible for how it is stored, secured, and used. Review that operator’s own policies.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above when we do and, for significant changes, may provide additional notice. If you keep using Snippy after changes take effect, you accept the updated policy.

16. Contact

Privacy questions and requests, including parent or guardian requests, can be sent through the Snippy GitHub repository. Please do not post personal information, or information about a child, in a public issue. Ask for a private contact method instead.

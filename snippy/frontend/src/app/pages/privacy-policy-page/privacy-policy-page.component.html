Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Snippy (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is an open-source tool for creating, previewing, and sharing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript snippets. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Snippy and how that information is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, authentication is handled by the PocketBase instance that hosts this copy of Snippy. We store identifiers and profile details needed to create and maintain your account (such as a unique user id, email, display name, username, bio, and profile picture).
  • Content you create. Snippets, collections, comments, tags, external resource URLs, favorites, and related metadata you save in Snippy.
  • Usage data. Basic operational data such as view counts and similar engagement signals associated with public content.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs (for example IP address, timestamps, and request metadata) may be collected by the host running your Snippy instance for security and reliability.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and improve Snippy features (editing, previewing, sharing, following, favorites, comments, and collections).
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To show public profile and public snippet content to other users according to your privacy settings.
  • To diagnose issues, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

Public vs private content

Snippets and collections can be marked public or private. Public content may be visible to other users and appear in public feeds. Private content is intended to be visible only to you. Do not put secrets, credentials, or personal data you are not comfortable sharing into public snippets.

Third-party services

Self-hosted Snippy authenticates users with PocketBase on the same origin. Optional social login (for example GitHub) is configured by the operator. Uploaded avatars, assets, and snapshots are stored in that instance’s PocketBase data volume.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account and content data while your account remains active and as needed to operate the service. You may update profile details in Settings. Where account deletion is available in the product, requesting deletion removes or anonymizes associated personal data subject to backup and legal retention requirements of the operator hosting the instance.

Self-hosted deployments

Snippy is open source and may be hosted by third parties. If you are using a self-hosted or third-party instance, that operator is responsible for how data is stored, secured, and processed on their infrastructure. Review that operator’s policies in addition to this document.

Children’s privacy

Snippy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of Snippy after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about this policy can be raised via the Snippy GitHub repository.
